What SeaBathy is

SeaBathy is a free tool for exploring the seabed, planning trips and fishing. It shows public depth surveys, models and forecasts on a map. It is for research, planning, education and general awareness.

It is not an official nautical chart, an electronic chart display (ECDIS) or a safety-of-life system, and it has not been checked or approved by any hydrographic office or maritime authority.

The data

Everything the apps show comes from public sources: government and university depth surveys, global depth models, weather and ocean models, satellite data, marine park zoning, buoy and beacon lists, place names and OpenStreetMap. Each source is credited in the apps.

  • Depths may be from old surveys, coarse models or estimates, and the seabed changes. Shoals, rocks, wrecks and other hazards may be missing or in the wrong place.
  • Tides are modelled, not an official tide table.
  • Forecasts are model output and can be wrong. Always check the latest official forecast and warnings.
  • Buoys, beacons and lights may have moved, been removed or be missing, and some types are guessed from the data.
  • Marine park zones are simplified copies of the official zoning and can lag behind changes. The official zoning map and rules for each park are the law.
