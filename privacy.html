Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

This policy covers the SeaBathy apps (SeaBathy for Android, SeaBathy Desktop, BathyPicker and AT5 Chart Maker) and this website. Last updated 14 November 2026.

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. The apps have no analytics at all; this website counts visits anonymously, without cookies (see below).
  • SeaBathy has no servers of its own. Nothing is sent to us, and we never see your location, files, waypoints or routes.
  • To show maps and ocean data, the apps ask public services for the area you're looking at. Those requests are described below.
  • Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your GPS position, the survey and chart files you open, waypoints, routes, tracks, anchor watches, tide places, saved maps and settings are kept on your phone or computer only. They are not backed up to the cloud by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes them (files you opened from your own storage stay where they are).

Location

The Android app asks for location permission so it can show your boat on the chart, record tracks, run the anchor alarm and give depths along your route. Your position is used on the device. With your permission it is also used while the app is in the background, but only while you have a track recording or the anchor alarm on, shown by a notification you can stop at any time.

When you use "Following you" for tides, or ask for sea conditions where you are, the app sends that position (rounded to about 10 m) to the tide and forecast services below, as it would for any other spot you pick on the map. This only happens when you ask for that information.

What the apps send to other services

These are public services the apps use to fetch maps and data. Each request includes the map area or point concerned, and, like any internet request, your device's IP address. The apps identify themselves to these services by name ("SeaBathy" or "BathyPicker"). They send no names, accounts, files, advertising IDs or device identifiers. AT5 Chart Maker works entirely offline and contacts nothing.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and may keep ordinary server logs. SeaBathy has no control over them and receives nothing from them about you.

Debug log

The app keeps a short log of what it did, on the device, so problems can be fixed. Web addresses in it have keys and passwords removed. It never leaves the device unless you choose "Export debug log" and share it yourself.

This website

seabathy.com sets no cookies. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: a small script from Cloudflare notes which page was viewed, the site you came from, your browser and device type, and your country, so we can see which pages people use. It sets no cookies, doesn't identify you, and doesn't follow you to other sites. The website is hosted by Cloudflare and the app downloads by GitHub.

Children

SeaBathy is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on SeaBathy's GitHub.
