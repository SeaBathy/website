How-to guide

From a box on the map to your boat.

Four free apps that work together. Get a depth file for the water you fish, look at it on your phone or PC, and put it on your chartplotter. SeaBathy and AT5 Chart Maker work anywhere in the world; BathyPicker's free data covers Australian waters.

1. Get a depth file

BathyPicker cuts a piece out of the IMAS seabed survey of all Australian waters (up to 10 m detail along the coast) and saves it as one GeoTIFF, the standard file for depth maps.

  1. Download BathyPicker: BathyPicker.exe for Windows, BathyPicker.apk for Android, or the Python file for macOS and Linux. Downloads.
  2. Drag the box over the water you want, by its round handle, and pull the corners. Or pick a place from Jump to.
  3. Press Check size. It shows the detail and the file size before anything downloads. Big boxes step down in detail by themselves.
  4. Press Build GeoTIFF. A bay takes seconds, a whole coastline a few minutes.

On a computer the file goes in bathy_exports in your user folder. On a phone it goes in Download/BathyPicker, and Open sends it straight to SeaBathy.

Outside Australia, or with a survey of your own: any GeoTIFF of depths, or an MBTiles chart, works in every step below. Many countries publish free surveys (for example NOAA in the US, or your national hydrographic office), and SeaBathy can download free worldwide depths (GMRT) for the area on screen: Settings → Get seabed depths for this area.

2. On your phone

SeaBathy for Android, Android 8 or newer. Download the APK on the phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser.

Open your depth file

  1. Menu (☰) → Settings → Load GeoTIFF (or Load MBTiles file).
  2. Pick the file. BathyPicker's are under This device → Download → BathyPicker. The map flies to it.
  3. In the Layers drawer, tap Colours and choose a preset (reef and shallows, inshore, coastal, offshore), or set your own depth range.
  4. Tap 3D view to fly over it. Raise the height to 10× or more and the reef walls and channels stand out. Tap anywhere for the depth and slope.

Before you leave the ramp

  • Offline maps: Settings → Offline maps → Download the area on screen. Satellite imagery for when there's no signal.
  • Tides: tap the Tide chip, choose a place and save the weeks ahead.
  • Sea conditions: Settings → Sea conditions. Wind, waves, swell, currents, sea temperature and its breaks, chlorophyll, and temperature at a depth you choose, for 200 km round your spot. Tap a line in the values box to show it on the map.

On the water

  • Routes: tap the route button and tap the chart. Legs shows the least survey depth on each leg against your draft. Saved routes → Go navigates one, with off-course and shallow-water alarms.
  • Waypoints: the waypoint button drops a mark at the crosshair. Add an icon, colour and notes.
  • Anchor alarm: Settings → Anchor alarm → Drop anchor at the boat, and set the circle. It keeps watching with the screen off.
  • Tracks: Settings → Tracks → Start recording. Share it later as GPX.
  • GPX: Settings → Import GPX and Export waypoints and route, to swap marks with your plotter or PC.

3. On your PC

SeaBathy Desktop is the same app for Windows 10 and 11, as one portable exe with nothing to install. The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway.

  1. Drag the GeoTIFF or MBTiles onto the window.
  2. Colours sets the palette and depth range; 3D view opens it in 3D. Drag to turn, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. F fits it, T looks from the top.
  3. Plan routes on the big screen, then export GPX and import it on the phone or plotter.

Offline maps you save on the PC are MBTiles files in Documents\SeaBathy\OfflineDownloads. Copy them to the phone and load them there too.

4. On your chartplotter

AT5 Chart Maker (Windows) makes charts for Simrad, Lowrance and B&G units that read Insight charts. Unzip it and run AT5 Chart Maker.exe.

  1. Drag your depth GeoTIFF onto the window. It's recognised as a depth survey.
  2. Pick the colours and the Colour depths they spread over. A small range such as 0–30 m shows reefs in fine detail. Line depths limits the contour lines to a range of their own, such as 0–50 m. Make is set to the shaded picture and a contour chart; choose Shaded picture only or Contour chart only if you want just one. Lines every sets the contour spacing: leave it on auto, or type one such as 5.
  3. Press Convert. You get a shaded depth picture and a contour chart, as .at5 files named after the chart.
  4. Copy the .at5 files to the top of an SD card (FAT32, 32 GB or smaller), not inside a folder, and put it in the plotter. Keep Also put the name in the file names ticked, so several charts can sit together.
  5. On the plotter, choose Lowrance / Insight as the chart source. On Simrad, turn on Chart options → Photo overlay → Full to see the depth picture.

AT5 Chart Maker also converts contour MBTiles and satellite pictures. Every setting has a ? that explains it. The full manual is in HOW-TO.md.

If something's not right

  • The file is empty or patchy. IMAS maps the seafloor inside Australian waters only, so land and some offshore gaps have no data. Check the coverage BathyPicker reports when it finishes.
  • The file is too big. Use a smaller box, or set a coarser detail in BathyPicker. Phones handle files of a few hundred MB easily, and big ones on 64-bit phones.
  • Nothing shows on the plotter. Check the chart source is Lowrance / Insight, Photo overlay is on, and the .at5 files are at the top of the card, not in a folder: on the Simrad AT5 Chart Maker was tested on, charts in folders weren't found. Try a small chart first.
  • The chart shows but the colours don't. On Simrad, Photo overlay must be Full: Land only hides the picture over water.
  • Still stuck? Open an issue on the app's GitHub page and say what you tried.
